At MedTrust Clinic, our mission is to redefine the standard of community healthcare through unwavering dedication to patient well-being, clinical excellence, and compassionate service. We believe that true healing begins with listening and understanding.

Leadership & Vision

Meet Our Lead Specialist

Dr. Julian Sterling serves as the Chief Medical Officer at MedTrust Clinic, bringing over 25 years of distinguished experience in internal medicine and clinical research. A graduate of Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine, Dr. Sterling has dedicated his career to the pursuit of medical excellence and the refinement of patient-centered care models.

Throughout his tenure, he has pioneered several integrative health initiatives that focus on preventative wellness rather than reactive treatment. His philosophy is rooted in the belief that every patient deserves a dedicated advocate in their health journey—someone who treats the whole person, not just the symptoms.

Under his leadership, MedTrust Clinic has grown into a regional leader in specialized diagnostics and community wellness. Dr. Sterling continues to mentor the next generation of physicians, emphasizing that while technology is a tool, compassion is the foundation of the healing arts.
