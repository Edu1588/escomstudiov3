Phase 02 • Current Website Audit

Clinic Website Diagnostics: chiroandmovement.com

We ran a comprehensive audit of your current online setup and identified 4 critical optimization gaps that are actively deterring high-ticket patients and draining your conversion potential.

Weebly platform limits responsiveness & speed

The site is hosted on Weebly, generating sluggish load times, limiting local SEO visibility, and preventing clear, straightforward booking for local patients.

Dr. Karen's bio and authority are buried on home

Dr. Karen Maley's credentials are secondary. Her biography and certifications are nested in hard-to-reach subpages instead of the main page fold, failing to build trust with the local community.

No prominent floating booking CTAs

While the clinic leverages Janeapp for bookings, there are no floating booking elements that stay with the user as they scroll on mobile, leading to a conversion loss of up to 35%.

Lack of visible testimonials and recovery proof

For a local neighborhood clinic, visible proof of recovery and social testimonials right on the home page flow are vital to establish clinical trust before scheduling.
