Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is a chiropractic adjustment?

A chiropractic adjustment is a precise, controlled force applied to a spinal joint to restore proper alignment, improve range of motion, and relieve nerve pressure/back pain.

Are chiropractic treatments safe?

Yes, chiropractic care is widely recognized as one of the safest drug-free, non-invasive therapies available for back pain, neck pain, and joint issues.

How many sessions will I need?

This depends on your condition, pain level, and lifestyle. After your initial evaluation, our clinical team will outline a personalized treatment plan for fast relief.

Does the chiropractic adjustment hurt?

Generally, adjustments are painless. Many patients experience immediate relief or a feeling of release. Some mild soreness may occur afterwards, similar to starting a new exercise.

Can I seek chiropractic care if I am pregnant?

Absolutely! Chiropractic adjustments are safe and beneficial during pregnancy, helping to balance the pelvis, reduce lower back stress, and improve overall alignment.

Do you accept health insurance or payment plans?

We work with many major insurance providers and offer transparent self-pay rates and convenient family package plans to keep your spine care affordable.
